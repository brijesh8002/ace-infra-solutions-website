PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Is Important To Us

ACE Infra Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, enquiry forms and other communication channels.

Last Updated: September 2026
01

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request a quotation, submit an enquiry or communicate with our team.

  • Full name
  • Company or organisation name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City or project location
  • Project requirements and other information you provide
02

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to us may be used to understand your requirements and provide appropriate business services.

  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Prepare and provide project quotations
  • Communicate regarding our services
  • Coordinate project-related requirements

03

Information Sharing

ACE Infra Solutions does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared when reasonably required to provide a requested service, operate our website, comply with applicable legal requirements or protect our legitimate business interests.

04

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect the information provided to us against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

05

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage and provide a better browsing experience.

You may adjust your browser settings to control or disable cookies. Some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

06

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with personal information.

07

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

08

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised date.
